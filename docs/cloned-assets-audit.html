Useful assets we already have access to

Compared the TI4 bot and web clones with our current public assets, draft catalog and display components. This is a shortlist for choosing the next changes; production assets and application code are unchanged by this audit.

Start with shaped planet traits, Twilight’s Fall category glyphs, and speaker/commodity symbols. The next larger improvement is expanding Original card popups.

Planet traits

Strong candidate

Distinct shapes would make cultural, industrial and hazardous planets recognizable without relying only on dot color. Retain the mixed-trait display and hover/tap labels. The bot has larger emoji versions; the web clone has compact versions.

Twilight’s Fall categories

Strong candidate

Dedicated ability and genome glyphs plus the TI unit-upgrade symbol. These could accompany the category labels on bag cards while keeping the faction icon as the main identity. Our fallback currently uses generic sparkles, DNA and rocket icons.

Speaker and economy

Strong candidate

The speaker token fits the speaker/first-seat choice. Commodity and trade-good icons would clarify commodity counts on king and faction cards; they are separate assets and should retain distinct labels.

Wormholes

Compare before replacing

The bot has complete alpha, beta, gamma, delta and epsilon tokens, including the surrounding swirl. Our small icons are simplified letter-and-ring drawings. Its epsilon token is yellow; our current epsilon is red. Keep the compact option where the original raster token would become hard to read.

Leader portraits and roles

Strong candidate

The web clone contains 99 portrait files totaling 843 KiB; 92 directly match our catalog IDs: 32 agents, 30 commanders and 30 heroes. Portraits can supplement faction identity in leader cards. The bot also has generic I / II / III role symbols. Keep Twilight’s Fall genome art for genome cards.

Other colored units

Useful for fleet displays

All 11 standard unit types exist in each of the eight king colors. We already imported the flagship and mech pairs; carriers, cruisers, dreadnoughts, destroyers, fighters, infantry, PDS, space docks and war suns can use the same approach. Apply a color only when the player or king color is known.

Original card art

Largest content gap

These are existing imageURL references in bot JSON, resolved to files in the clone and matched to our catalog IDs. Our importer currently limits ordinary image imports mostly to unit upgrades, plus Twilight’s Fall cards. Extending it could provide Original card popups for many more entries. Preserve catalog text and errata as the active rules.

Card backs and source badges

Optional polish

Twilight’s Fall card backs are available for any future concealed-card or deck views. Expansion badges could accompany the source labels already present in card footers. These are less urgent than improving existing symbols and reference popups.

What to retain or treat separately

  • Our existing faction reference cards already reproduce much of the quick_reference artwork. The inspected Arborec card is visually the same TE design at a different resolution. The bot also has 61 PoK and 67 TE reference sheets; match the expansion before choosing replacements.
  • Keep faction icons, existing tile art and the newly imported resource / king assets. No blanket replacement is needed.
  • The web app often loads images through images.asyncti4.com and references generated sprite sheets. Reuse local bot files for this app; its sprite CSS is not a standalone local asset bundle.
  • Ordinary faction abilities, promissory notes, plots and monuments did not yield additional card fronts through the direct imageURL matching used for this audit. This does not prove artwork is absent under every other name.
