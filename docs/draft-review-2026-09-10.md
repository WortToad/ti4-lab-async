# Draft logic and experience review — 10 September 2026

The app covers most of the bot's main drafting mechanics. This review identified problems in setup, preservation of unfinished choices, special-case faction setup, and map generation. The fixes below retain format-specific rules while making the shared lobby experience consistent.

This review compares the working app against the local `TI4_map_generator_bot` checkout at commit `690bb6935d497e81667b6f59e00b0aa51902d858`. It includes source inspection, engine and persistence tests, and a browser check of the shared lobby on desktop and mobile. It is not an exhaustive verification of every faction, expansion combination, or game rule. The bot checkout was not changed.

**What agrees with the bot**

- Franken, Powered, One-pick, Overdraft, FrankenDraz and Twilight's Fall bag quotas, pass direction, category restrictions, and generic technology substitutions agree with the corresponding bot implementations. See `FrankenDraft.java:57`, `FrankenDrazDraft.java:73`, `TwilightsFallFrankenDraft.java:12`, and `BagDraft.java:85` under the bot's `src/main/java/ti4/draft` directory.
- Mantis uses the bot's inner/middle/outer placement groups, speaker-order tie breaks, extra-tile discards, and mulligans that retain the original tile. These rules should be preserved.
- RAW has its own ring and placement restrictions. Sharing card and lobby components is useful; merging RAW, Mantis and Texas placement rules would erase meaningful differences.
- Nucleus layout support does not provide the bot's complete Nucleus generation controls. Per-nucleus values, spread limits and additional placement settings remain documented differences. Custom bot decks, some homebrew packs, and Discord game execution also remain outside the app's scope; see [the feature comparison](draft-parity.md).

**Final fixes from the remaining review items**

| Issue | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Texas undersized hands and redraws | Setup and dealers reject insufficient faction/tile supply. Full-hand and all-player-redraw regressions cover the original failures. Exhausted or disabled legacy redraw requests now fail atomically instead of silently assigning the first discarded faction. |
| Keleres in Mantis | An explicit home-and-hero step offers legal unplayed Mentak, Xxcha and Argent choices. Drafting prevents the conflicting quartet, and constrained pools cannot strand a player. The choice is protected by player ownership and supports undo, backup and recovery. Legacy unresolved maps reopen only the home decision without losing placements; valid existing homes stay intact. |
| Keleres in standard and Texas drafts | Final results request the Keleres player's home and matching hero before completing map exports. Legal choices account for played primary/minor factions and occupied homes on the prepared map; the decision persists and can be undone. Standard faction and slice choices preserve a feasible completion across shared, separate and preassigned pools. Creation and post-ban generation validate actual dealt pools with bounded retries. Incompatible saved or simultaneous Texas results show checkpoint/undo recovery guidance. |
| Mini-Milty reroll | The preview uses its own complete-map generator, retains faction edits, and resets to the originally prepared map. Regression tests cover all supported player counts. |
| Impossible slice recipes | A supply-aware weighted sampler chooses only recipes that leave enough tiles for the remaining slices. Generation resamples feasible recipes with bounded search and rejects impossible supply/global limits promptly. Seeded tests replace the long unseeded stress loops. Additional fixes cover missing configuration, duplicate systems, exhausted map pools, and overwritten shared core cells. |
| Unsubmitted bag choices | Picks and assembly edits persist in the current tab, scoped to the lobby and a hash of the current player identity. Restoration checks phase, hand, pool, rules and legality. Spectators, replaced identities, completed submissions and stale contexts cannot reuse those choices. |
| Turn awareness | Standard, Texas, reference-pack, bag, Mantis and RAW drafts share pending-action status, a tab-title indicator, and optional sound/browser alerts. Simultaneous rounds stop alerting after the player's choice is submitted. Alerts are deduplicated through refresh and requested only through an explicit control. Lobby data refreshes on visibility, reconnect and online events; opted-in alerts keep background tabs up to date. |
| Draft refreshes | Standard same-room updates preserve open controls and replay position; simultaneous picks and undo wait for authenticated router revalidation. Across standard, bag, Mantis and RAW, response ordering prevents delayed pre-pick reads from replacing newer state or restoring a previous player's private view. |
| Multidraft preparation | Each lobby respects configured faction pools, priorities, quotas and prepared maps. The entire batch is generated and validated before any lobby is saved, and preparation failures explain how to return to setup. |
| Faction bans | Validation checks the source used after bans, including private-hand requirements and separate minor pools. Individual bans cannot exhaust a required expansion quota; disabled choices explain why, and the server enforces the same rule. |
| Minor faction choices | Keleres has no fixed minor home, so minor-only pools and choices exclude it while preserving its availability as a primary faction. Exhausted minor pools cannot add an undefined entry. |
| RAW on phones | The active game container fits the viewport while wide player tables scroll within their own container. Choosing a faction no longer scrolls the whole page sideways. The duplicate invite control has been removed; sharing remains in the common lobby. |
| Long lists and forced placements | Bag selected counts and submit/finalize controls stay reachable while scrolling. Mantis automatically records a forced placement only when there is one legal position and no legal mulligan; individual snapshots keep it undoable. Loading, resuming or undoing does not immediately repeat the automatic move. |

These implement the bug fixes and QOL changes listed in this review. Browser alerts still require an open tab; delivery after closing the application would require a separate push service. Incompatible old faction selections, or simultaneous Texas choices that occupy all three Keleres homes, require the admin to restore or undo to a legal selection. Export remains blocked until resolved; private staged choices stay private. The broader bot feature boundaries, such as Discord game execution and unbundled custom decks, remain described in [the feature comparison](draft-parity.md).

**Validation**

The production build and TypeScript checks pass. The complete suite passes **497 tests across 53 files**, with no exclusions. This includes the repaired Milty-EQ, five-player Milty-EQ and Nucleus generator suites; the former unseeded 10,000-iteration loops are now deterministic regressions for completeness, uniqueness, supply and configured constraints. Tests also cover ownership, private projections, checkpoints, undo, pending choices, refresh ordering, and faction completion across shared, separate and preassigned pools.

Browser checks cover player-count and format continuity, template feedback, Texas shortages, unnamed preview creation, joining and starting, recovery controls, tournament creation, and desktop/mobile layout. Final production checks confirm Mini-Milty map/faction edits survive reload, reset preserves faction changes, failed creation retains a retryable preview, and successful creation clears it. RAW checks hold an old response across a submission, verify it cannot restore the previous choice, and exercise waiting status, explicit alert opt-in, paused undo/resume, background notifications and refresh deduplication. Keleres checks verify legal home cards, blocked exports until selection, persisted completion, mobile width, and undo of only the home decision. Separate browser checks cover saved bag picks and assembly edits, Mantis home selection, and standard open controls surviving background refresh.

All **140 changed TypeScript/JavaScript files pass lint**, and whitespace checks pass. SQLite tests use Node 22 to match the installed native module. Browser fixtures use temporary databases; the bot checkout was not changed.
